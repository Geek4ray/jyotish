- **Where:** `src/jyotish/narrate.py:2277` (`engine_line`), called by `instant_reading` (2292) from

### R2 — high — Every review and comparison write now accepts a pending reading, and the job's final write discards it

- **Where:**
  - `src/jyotish/api.py:461-469` (`done_reading` now admits `running`). This is the one helper behind
    `post_answer` (562), `post_edit` (601), `post_note` (633), `post_verdict` (652), `post_approve` (667)

- **What is wrong:** the request was "the review page can open it". The change opens every write, but the
  job's final `update(..., document=reading, page=page)` replaces the row's document wholesale, whatever a
  reviewer saved in between. Nothing locks or versions the row (`ReadingRow` has no version column).
- **Failure scenarios (reproduced offline, model fixture, the job held after its instant page):**
  - A note posted on the pending reading returns 200. After the job finishes, the area's `astrologer_note`
    is gone.
  - Answers for every area return 200, and `POST /review/approve` returns 200 "reviewed". After the job
    finishes, the row says `review_status: reviewed` while the stored document says `review.status: draft`.
    The final narration (8 claims) was approved by a reviewer who saw none of it.
- **Failure scenarios that follow from the code (not run, since they would call the live checker or
  race the job):**
  - `post_edit` on the engine's summary runs the live checker (it costs a call) and its accepted edit is
    then lost.
  - A blind comparison can be opened and answered on two instant pages, so the judgement and the revealed
    settings (empty `writer` on an instant document) are logged against readings that do not exist yet.
  - On Postgres (read committed), a note or edit transaction
    that read the instant document and commits just after the job's final update writes the instant page
    back over the finished narration. The row then stays `done` with `narration_status: "pending"`, and the
    site shows "being prepared" forever.
- **What a fix needs:**
  - Open the review view for a running reading, but refuse every write (answer, edit, note, verdict,
    approve, compare) until `status == "done"` and `narration_status == "done"`.
  - Or merge reviewer state into the final document and make the job's write conditional on the row's
    version.
  - Tests for each refused write on a pending reading.

### R7 — medium — Each live viewer pins a server worker thread, and the page keeps re-downloading the 200 KB instant document

- **Where:** `src/jyotish/api.py:889-915` (sync generator over `LIVE.wait(..., timeout=15)` under
  `StreamingResponse`); `web/js/reading-page.js:71` with `api.js:31` (polling continues while
  `follow` runs); `api.py:864` (a running reading returns its document and page).
- **What is wrong:**
  - Threads: Starlette runs a sync iterator through `iterate_in_threadpool`. Each open SSE connection
    holds one of anyio's 40 default worker threads almost continuously, for the whole narration. Every
    sync endpoint (GET/POST /readings, review, charts) shares those threads.
  - Polling: `pollReading` keeps calling GET /readings/{id} every 1.5 s while the page follows the SSE.
    Each response now carries the instant document (113,612 bytes) plus its page (90,723 bytes) on the
    reference chart. Before this batch a running reading returned only its progress.
- **Failure scenarios:**
  - About 40 open reading pages, or one script opening 40 SSE connections to a reading it posted (no
    auth or limit on /events), stall the whole site on the one-machine Fly deployment until those
    narrations end.
  - A single viewer of a 40-minute narration pulls about 1,600 × 204 KB (about 330 MB) of identical JSON.
  - `LiveLog.append`'s `notify_all` wakes every waiting stream for any reading's event.
- **What a fix needs:**
  - An async generator, or an `asyncio`-side wait, for /events, with a cap on connections per reading.
  - Stop the full-document poll while SSE is live, or return progress only for a running reading after

### R8 — low — Live-view bookkeeping: stale units, uncounted checks, loose verdict matching, log eviction

- **Where:** `src/jyotish/live.py` (`draft_stream`'s reset at 301-305, `_verdict` at 323-347, `SharedChecker`
  at 360-405, `LiveLog.append` at 421-428); `src/jyotish/narrate.py:2606-2632` (`early`).
- **What is wrong:**
  - **Stale units.** These stay "being verified" until the final redraw, which can be 20 to 40 minutes,
    with no `removed`:
    - a unit shown by a stream that was dropped and restarted (`on_text(None)` resets the JSON reader, not
      the `Live` state);
    - a claim with an unknown finding id;
    - a unit missing from the final parse.
  - **Uncounted checks.** An early check the loop never consumes is a real provider call, but it is left
    out of `usage.checker`, the ledger cost and the recording. This happens with:
    - the closed units of a dropped stream;
    - a duplicate claim;
    - a JSON-mode schema retry;
    - a stream text that differs from the parsed text (JSON surrogate pairs decode differently in
      `JsonStrings`).

    The same holds when a key is consumed by another unit's `check_many` and re-asked.
  - **Loose verdict matching.**
    - For summary, paragraph and analysis units a verdict marks or removes every shown sentence that is a
      *substring* of the checked one (`x in sentence`). A stale verdict on "As the KP vote shows, Ketu
      decides …" can verify or remove a rewritten "Ketu decides …".
    - Cross-area sharing of one future (identical sentence and fact data, e.g. a timing sentence citing
      only `[fact:vimshottari.current]` in two areas) can show "verified" from one sample while the loop
      decides on another.
  - **Log eviction.** `LiveLog` evicts the oldest log at 200 even while its reading runs. The next append
    restarts `seq` at 1, and a client whose version is higher then drops every later event.
- **What a fix needs:**
  - On a stream reset, clear or remove the units it showed.
  - Remove units the loop never decides on.
  - Record and count every check the pool runs.
  - Match verdicts by exact sentence identity.
  - Never evict a running reading's log.

## Checked and found sound

- **`JsonStrings`:**
  - nested objects and arrays, empty lists and objects, literals, escapes (`\"`, `\\`, `\/`, `\n`,
    `\uXXXX`) and chunk boundaries anywhere;
  - a prefix of the stream always decodes to a prefix of the final string (surrogate pairs excepted, R8);
  - `feed(None)` resets the reader.
- **The bc1c03a fix:**
  - a refused text seen again (the loop after an early verdict, or an unchanged retry) is not re-shown;
  - a removed-then-accepted unit is re-shown with its text;
  - a late claim verdict for a replaced text is dropped;
  - the new test forces CI's order.
- **Locks and threads:** no lock-order inversion between `Live.lock` and `LiveLog.cond`. The stream, the
  loop and the verdict callbacks of one area cannot interleave so that the loop accepts text other than
  the text it checked. The early check and the loop share one future per (sentence, data), and the loop's
  decision is the one stored.
- **The stored reading, the Markdown page and the review page** hold only text that passed, or the
  engine's own. Fixed in R2 they would stay so. The final redraw on the site comes from GET /readings.
- **The instant page:** no model call is made (`_NoNarration` and a lambda checker, `max_attempts=1`, no
  client's layer); no gaps or "No claims" line; it is stored before the first writer call.
  POST → instant page is about 2 s (chart) plus 3 s on this machine.
- **SSE and polling:**
  - Last-Event-ID is preferred over `since`.
  - A synthetic `done` is sent when the log is gone and the row is finished.
  - `done` is appended after the row is updated, so the client's redraw reads the final document.
  - Polling dedupes by `seq`.
  - Blind comparisons get 409 on both endpoints.
  - No XSS in `live.js` (everything through `esc`, selectors through `CSS.escape`).
- **`e4bf114`:** in-flight checker tasks are cancelled before the batch's client closes.
- **Groq:** a json_schema call is never streamed, and the JSON-mode fallback still covers a server that
  refuses a streamed schema.
- **Temperature:** sent only when set, and recorded in `describe()`.
- **Keys:** no key reaches a log line, an event, a reading, a recording or a committed file. The new
  scripts print `.env` names only, and the retry log carries host and model only.
- **The offline suite for the batch passes.** My own stub runs at curious and full depth completed: the
  per-area client's layer, the client's-layer summary at full and the `SummaryDraft` summary at curious
  all worked (apart from R3).
